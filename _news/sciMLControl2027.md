<p>
  The <b>Scientific Machine Learning and Control for Intelligent Industrial Systems</b> workshop brings together academia and industry to explore advances in scientific machine learning and control, including physics-based and data-driven modeling, reduced-order modeling, optimization, and digital twins. By fostering bidirectional knowledge exchange and addressing industrial challenges as drivers of scientific innovation, the workshop aims to identify shared research priorities, stimulate collaborative projects, and strengthen academia–industry partnerships.
  The workshop is organized and led by Working Group 4 of the COST Action InterCoML, and takes place in <b>Milan, Italy</b>, from <b>February 3 to February 5, 2027</b>.
  More information and the registration form can be found on the <a href="https://www.mate.polimi.it/events/InterCoMLWG4/index.php#home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="link">workshop homepage</a>.
