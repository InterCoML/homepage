<h4>Abstract of the STSM:</h4>
<p>
Optimal control problems are ubiquitous in engineering and science. However, solving them efficiently, especially for <b>parameter-dependent systems</b>, remains computationally demanding. Machine learning (ML) has emerged as a promising tool for accelerating these solutions, but rigorous <b>error certification</b> is required when approximating the optimal control in safety-critical applications.
</p>
<p>
The STSM focused on an <b>ML-enhanced optimal control framework</b> for constrained optimal control problems involving the penalization of deviation from a <b>desired trajectory</b> which is particularly challenging to solve using classical numerical methods. 
</p>
<p>
The follow-up research activity will focus on deriving suitable expressions for the optimal control to make the problem amenable to a <b>physics-informed neural network (PINN)</b> approach with <b>rigorous error bounds</b>. The PINN will then provide an approximate solution to the Lyapunov differential equation, enabling the optimal control for a specific parameter and target state to be computed easily.
</p>
<p>
Unlike conventional reduced-basis methods, the proposed project will develop an approach that does not require data generated using classical numerical solvers. This strategy promises to <b>reduce offline computational costs</b> and <b>accelerate online costs</b> significantly.
</p>
